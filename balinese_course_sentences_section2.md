# Balinese App Sentence Curriculum — Section 2: Building Sentences (A1.1–A1.2)

> **Companion file to:** `balinese_course_vocabulary.md` (Lessons 11–30) and `balinese_course_sentences_section1.md`
> **Format:** `Balinese sentence -> English translation`
>
> **Same construction rules as Section 1:**
> 1. Every vocabulary entry of a lesson appears in **at least 3 sentences** within that lesson (entries recycled from earlier lessons get 3 fresh sentences too).
> 2. Each sentence introduces **at most ONE new vocabulary entry**; all other words come from the current or previous lessons (cumulative from Lesson 1).
> 3. Register consistency: *Alus* pairs with *Alus/Mider*, *Andap* with *Andap/Mider*.
> 4. Multi-word entries (`rumah sakit`, `jalan lurus`, `be celeng`, `tiang jagi numbas niki`…) count as one entry.
> 5. Proper names (Wayan, Made, …, Gunung Agung) are placeholders, not vocabulary.
> 6. Grammatical glue: suffix **-ne/-né**, yes/no question **-a?**, linker **-ng** inside number words (`telung dasa`).
>
> **Grammar unlocked in Section 2 (not before!):**
> - **Lesson 12:** prepositions `di`/`ring` (at/in) and `ka` (to) — before this, location was shown with `ada/wénten` + place word.
> - **Lesson 14:** time words (`jani`, `dibi`, `mani`…) — these also carry future/past meaning, because the future marker `lakar` only arrives in **Lesson 21** and Alus `jagi` (standalone) even later.
> - **Lesson 15:** the adjective toolbox (`gede`, `cenik`, `jegeg`…) — adjectives follow the noun: `umah gede` "big house".
> - **Lesson 26:** `ajak`/`sareng`/`teken` (with) — before this, "together" ideas use `lan`/`miwah`.
> - **Lesson 30:** full conjunction system (`krana`, `yening`, `sedurung`, `sesampun`…).

### Lesson 11: Verbs 1 — Routine

**Focus:** the daily-routine verbs (sleep, wake, bathe, work, sit, stand, walk, run, go home) in Andap/Alus pairs, "at home", and speed adverbs.
**Grammar note:** Balinese verbs don't change for person or tense — `Icang pules.` = "I sleep / I slept / I am sleeping"; context words like `suba` (already) and `dibi` (yesterday, Lesson 14) fix the time.
**New in this lesson (teaching order):** pules, sirep, medem, mesare, bangun, matangi, mandi, maseh, manjus, masiram, megawe, makarya, pagawean, pakaryan, negak, malinggih, ngadeg, majan, majalan (recycled), memargi (recycled), malaib, mlayu, jumah, mulih, mantuk, enggal, gati, gelis, adeng, alon
*(Notes: sleep verbs carry speech levels — `pules` neutral/familiar, `medem` for yourself politely, `mesare`/`sirep` for people you respect. `majan` is listed as "to stand" (familiar); most speakers use `ngadeg` — treat `majan` as a dialect variant. `gati` is glossed "fast" here, but as already seen in Lesson 5 (`jaen gati` "very delicious") it also works as the intensifier "very"; both uses appear below.)*

1. Pules. -> To sleep (familiar/neutral).
2. Icang pules. -> I sleep.
3. Ia suba pules. -> He is already asleep.
4. Cai pules dini? -> Do you sleep here?
5. Sirep. -> To sleep (said of someone you respect).
6. Ida sirep. -> He / she (honored) sleeps.
7. Ida durung sirep. -> He / she (honored) is not asleep yet.
8. Ipun sirep drika. -> He / she sleeps there.
9. Medem. -> To sleep (for oneself, polite).
10. Tiang medem. -> I sleep / I go to sleep.
11. Tiang medem dini. -> I sleep here.
12. Tiang sampun medem. -> I have already slept.
13. Mesare. -> To sleep (refined).
14. Ipun mesare. -> He / she sleeps.
15. Ipun mesare driki. -> He / she sleeps here.
16. Ragane mesare? -> Are you sleeping?
17. Bangun. -> To wake up (familiar).
18. Icang bangun. -> I wake up.
19. Cai suba bangun? -> Are you awake already?
20. Ia bangun semeng. -> He wakes up in the morning.
21. Matangi. -> To wake up (refined).
22. Ida matangi. -> He / she (honored) wakes up.
23. Ida matangi semeng. -> He / she (honored) wakes up in the morning.
24. Ipun sampun matangi. -> He / she is already awake.
25. Mandi. -> To bathe (familiar).
26. Icang mandi. -> I bathe.
27. Cai mandi semeng? -> Do you bathe in the morning?
28. Ia suba mandi. -> He has already bathed.
29. Maseh. -> To bathe (familiar variant).
30. Icang maseh semeng. -> I bathe in the morning.
31. Nyai maseh? -> Do you bathe? (to a woman)
32. Ia maseh, suba peteng. -> He bathes — it's already night.
33. Manjus. -> To bathe (familiar variant).
34. Icang manjus. -> I bathe.
35. Cai manjus tengai? -> Do you bathe at midday?
36. Ia manjus dini. -> He bathes here.
37. Masiram. -> To bathe (refined).
38. Ida masiram. -> He / she (honored) bathes.
39. Ipun masiram semeng. -> He / she bathes in the morning.
40. Ida durung masiram. -> He / she (honored) has not bathed yet.
41. Megawe. -> To work (familiar).
42. Icang megawe. -> I work.
43. Cai megawe dini? -> Do you work here?
44. Ia suba megawe. -> He has already worked.
45. Makarya. -> To work (refined).
46. Tiang makarya. -> I work.
47. Ida makarya. -> He / she (honored) works.
48. Ipun makarya driki. -> He / she works here.
49. Pagawean. -> Work / job (familiar noun).
50. Pagawean icang liu. -> My work is a lot.
51. Pagawean caine apa? -> What is your job?
52. Pagawean tiang becik. -> My job is good.
53. Pakaryan. -> Work / job (refined noun).
54. Pakaryan tiang becik. -> My work is good.
55. Pakaryan idane napi? -> What is his / her (honored) job?
56. Pakaryan ipun liu. -> His / her work is a lot.
57. Negak. -> To sit (familiar).
58. Icang negak dini. -> I sit here.
59. Cai negak ditu? -> Do you sit there?
60. Nyai negak dini, icang ditu. -> You sit here, I (sit) there.
61. Malinggih. -> To sit / to reside (refined).
62. Ida malinggih driki. -> He / she (honored) sits here.
63. Ragane malinggih drika? -> Do you sit there?
64. Ipun malinggih, nggih. -> He / she sits, yes.
65. Ngadeg. -> To stand (neutral).
66. Icang ngadeg. -> I stand.
67. Cai ngadeg ditu? -> Do you stand there?
68. Ia ngadeg dini, icang negak. -> He stands here, I sit.
69. Majan. -> To stand (familiar variant; see note).
70. Icang majan dini. -> I stand here.
71. Cai majan? -> Are you standing?
72. Ia majan ditu, nggih. -> He stands there, yes.
73. Majalan. -> To walk / to go (familiar). [recycled from Lesson 3]
74. Icang majalan. -> I walk.
75. Cai majalan kija? -> Where are you walking to?
76. Cai majalan, icang negak. -> You walk, I sit.
77. Memargi. -> To walk / to go (refined). [recycled from Lesson 3]
78. Tiang memargi. -> I walk / go.
79. Ida memargi drika. -> He / she (honored) walks there.
80. Ipun memargi, tiang malinggih. -> He / she walks, I sit.
81. Malaib. -> To run (familiar).
82. Icang malaib. -> I run.
83. Cicing ento malaib! -> That dog is running!
84. Cai malaib ditu? -> Do you run there?
85. Mlayu. -> To run (refined).
86. Ipun mlayu. -> He / she runs.
87. Asu nika mlayu driki. -> That dog runs here.
88. Wanara nika mlayu. -> That monkey runs.
89. Jumah. -> At home.
90. Icang jumah. -> I am at home.
91. Cai jumah? -> Are you at home?
92. Meme jumah, bapa megawe. -> Mother is at home, father works.
93. Mulih. -> To go home (familiar).
94. Icang mulih. -> I go home.
95. Cai mulih pidan? -> When do you go home?
96. Ia suba mulih. -> He has already gone home.
97. Mantuk. -> To go home (refined).
98. Tiang mantuk. -> I go home.
99. Ida mantuk. -> He / she (honored) goes home.
100. Ragane mantuk pidan? -> When do you go home?
101. Enggal. -> Fast / quick / soon (familiar).
102. Icang malaib enggal. -> I run fast.
103. Cai majalan enggal! -> Walk fast!
104. Ia mulih enggal. -> He goes home quickly.
105. Gati. -> Fast; very (see note).
106. Cicing nika malaib gati. -> That dog runs very fast.
107. Ia mlayu gati. -> He runs very fast.
108. Enggal gati! -> So fast!
109. Gelis. -> Fast (refined).
110. Ipun memargi gelis. -> He / she walks fast.
111. Ida mantuk gelis. -> He / she (honored) goes home quickly.
112. Ipun mlayu gelis. -> He / she runs fast.
113. Adeng. -> Slow (familiar).
114. Icang majalan adeng. -> I walk slowly.
115. Sampi nika majalan adeng. -> That cow walks slowly.
116. Cai malaib adeng? -> Do you run slowly?
117. Alon. -> Slow (refined).
118. Ipun memargi alon. -> He / she walks slowly.
119. Ida mantuk alon. -> He / she (honored) goes home slowly.
120. Lembu nika majalan alon. -> That cow walks slowly.

### Lesson 12: Places

**Focus:** locations around the village and town, plus the first true prepositions: `di`/`ring` (at/in) and `ka` (to), and the directionals `kema`/`mrika` (go there), `mai`/`mriki` (come here).
**Grammar note:** `di` (Andap) and `ring` (Alus) mark location; `ka` marks direction toward a place. `kema`/`mai` already contain the direction ("to there"/"to here"), so they need no `ka`.
**New in this lesson (teaching order):** di, ring, ka, tongos, genah, peken, pasar, warung, toko, kota, desa, banjar, pura, merajan, sanggah, sekolah, kantor, rumah sakit, margi, jalan (recycled — now also "street/road"), rurung, tukad, pasih, segara, alas, carik, kema, mrika, mai, mriki
*(Note: `jalan` from Lesson 3 ("let's go / to walk") also means "street/road" — context decides.)*

1. Di. -> At / in / on (familiar).
2. Icang di jumah. -> I am at home.
3. Cai di dija? -> Where are you?
4. Cicing ento di ditu. -> That dog is there.
5. Ring. -> At / in / on (refined).
6. Tiang ring jumah. -> I am at home.
7. Ida ring dija? -> Where is he / she (honored)?
8. Buku druwen tiang ring driki. -> My book is here.
9. Ka. -> To (a place).
10. Icang ka ditu. -> I (go) there.
11. Cai ka dija? -> Where are you going?
12. Ia ka Bali. -> He goes to Bali.
13. Tongos. -> Place (familiar).
14. Tongos icang dini. -> My place is here.
15. Tongos caine dija? -> Where is your place?
16. Tongos niki luung. -> This place is good.
17. Genah. -> Place (refined).
18. Genah tiang driki. -> My place is here.
19. Genah ida ring dija? -> Where is his / her (honored) place?
20. Genah niki becik. -> This place is good.
21. Peken. -> Market (familiar).
22. Icang ka peken. -> I go to the market.
23. Peken nika dija? -> Where is that market?
24. Meme majalan ka peken. -> Mother walks to the market.
25. Pasar. -> Market (refined).
26. Tiang ka pasar. -> I go to the market.
27. Pasar nika ring dija? -> Where is that market?
28. Biang memargi ka pasar. -> Mother walks to the market.
29. Warung. -> Small shop / food stall.
30. Ada warung dini. -> There is a food stall here.
31. Icang madaar di warung. -> I eat at the food stall.
32. Warung nika dija, cai? -> Where is that stall, mate?
33. Toko. -> Store.
34. Ada toko ditu. -> There is a store there.
35. Tiang ka toko. -> I go to the store.
36. Toko nika ring dija? -> Where is that store?
37. Kota. -> City.
38. Icang ka kota. -> I go to the city.
39. Tiang saking kota. -> I am from the city.
40. Kota nika liu toris. -> That city has many tourists.
41. Desa. -> Village.
42. Icang nongos di desa. -> I live in a village.
43. Desa ragane ring dija? -> Where is your village?
44. Desa niki luung. -> This village is good.
45. Banjar. -> Community hall / ward.
46. Ada banjar dini. -> There is a banjar here.
47. Icang ka banjar. -> I go to the banjar.
48. Banjar nika ring dija? -> Where is that banjar?
49. Pura. -> Temple.
50. Ada pura ditu. -> There is a temple there.
51. Ida memargi ka pura. -> He / she (honored) walks to the temple.
52. Pura nika ring dija? -> Where is that temple?
53. Merajan. -> Family temple.
54. Merajan wénten ring jero. -> The family temple is in the house compound.
55. Ada merajan di umah icang. -> There is a family temple at my house.
56. Merajan niki becik. -> This family temple is beautiful.
57. Sanggah. -> Family shrine (familiar).
58. Ada sanggah di jumah icang. -> There is a family shrine at my home.
59. Sanggah nika dija? -> Where is that shrine?
60. Sanggah lan merajan wénten dini. -> The shrine and the family temple are here.
61. Sekolah. -> School.
62. Panak icang ka sekolah. -> My child goes to school.
63. Sekolah nika dija? -> Where is that school?
64. Oka ida ring sekolah. -> His / her (honored) child is at school.
65. Kantor. -> Office.
66. Bapa icang megawe di kantor. -> My father works at the office.
67. Kantor nika dija? -> Where is that office?
68. Aji tiang makarya ring kantor. -> My father works at the office.
69. Rumah sakit. -> Hospital.
70. Ada rumah sakit dini. -> There is a hospital here.
71. Tiang ka rumah sakit. -> I go to the hospital.
72. Rumah sakit nika ring dija? -> Where is that hospital?
73. Ipun wénten ring rumah sakit. -> He / she is at the hospital.
74. Margi. -> Street / road (refined).
75. Margi nika becik. -> That road is good.
76. Ida memargi ring margi. -> He / she (honored) walks on the road.
77. Margi nika ring dija? -> Where is that road?
78. Jalan. -> Street / road (familiar); let's go. [recycled from Lesson 3]
79. Jalan niki luung. -> This road is good.
80. Icang majalan di jalan. -> I walk on the road.
81. Jalan nika dija? -> Where is that road?
82. Rurung. -> Alley / small street (familiar).
83. Ada rurung ditu. -> There is an alley there.
84. Icang majalan di rurung. -> I walk in the alley.
85. Rurung nika dija? -> Where is that alley?
86. Tukad. -> River.
87. Ada tukad dini. -> There is a river here.
88. Icang manjus di tukad. -> I bathe in the river.
89. Tukad nika ring dija? -> Where is that river?
90. Pasih. -> Beach / sea (familiar).
91. Ada pasih ditu. -> There is a beach there.
92. Icang majalan ka pasih. -> I walk to the beach.
93. Toris liu di pasih. -> There are many tourists at the beach.
94. Segara. -> Beach / sea (refined).
95. Wénten segara driki. -> There is a sea here.
96. Tiang memargi ka segara. -> I walk to the sea.
97. Segara nika becik pisan. -> That sea is very beautiful.
98. Alas. -> Forest.
99. Ada alas ditu. -> There is a forest there.
100. Bojog liu di alas. -> There are many monkeys in the forest.
101. Alas nika ring dija? -> Where is that forest?
102. Carik. -> Rice field.
103. Ada carik dini. -> There is a rice field here.
104. Bapa icang megawe di carik. -> My father works in the rice field.
105. Carik nika luung. -> That rice field is good.
106. Kema. -> Go there (familiar).
107. Icang kema. -> I go there.
108. Cai kema! -> You — go there!
109. Ia suba kema. -> He has already gone there.
110. Mrika. -> Go there (refined).
111. Tiang mrika. -> I go there.
112. Ida mrika, nggih. -> He / she (honored) goes there, yes.
113. Ragane mrika? -> Are you going there?
114. Mai. -> Come here (familiar).
115. Cai mai! -> Come here!
116. Icang mai. -> I come here.
117. Ia suba mai. -> He has already come here.
118. Mriki. -> Come here (refined).
119. Ragane mriki. -> You come here.
120. Tiang mriki. -> I come here.
121. Ida mriki, nggih. -> He / she (honored) comes here, yes.

### Lesson 13: Directions 1 (Balinese Spatial Orientation)

**Focus:** the Balinese directional system, up/down, front/behind, right/left, near/far, and giving simple directions.
**Cultural note:** Balinese orientation is not "north/south" first but **kaja** (toward the mountain — the sacred direction) and **kelod** (toward the sea), plus **kangin** (east, where the sun rises) and **kauh** (west). On Bali, kaja ≈ north and kelod ≈ south.
**New in this lesson (teaching order):** kaja, kangin, kelod, kauh, luhur, baduwur, beten, ring sor, ajeng, harep, pungkur, duri, tengen, kiwa, nampek, paek, adoh, joh, padoh, tanggu, tengah, sisi, jaba, jalan lurus, simpangan, dini (recycled), driki (recycled), ditu (recycled), drika (recycled)

1. Kaja. -> Toward the mountain / north (the sacred direction).
2. Pura nika ring kaja. -> That temple is to the north (up-island).
3. Umah pekak di kaja. -> Grandfather's house is to the north.
4. Kangin. -> East.
5. Sekolah nika ring kangin. -> That school is to the east.
6. Jero ida ring kangin. -> His / her (honored) home is to the east.
7. Pasar nika di kangin. -> That market is to the east.
8. Kelod. -> Toward the sea / south.
9. Pasih wénten ring kelod. -> The beach is to the south.
10. Umah icang di kelod. -> My house is to the south.
11. Segara nika ring kelod. -> That sea is to the south.
12. Kauh. -> West.
13. Toko nika di kauh. -> That store is to the west.
14. Kantor ida ring kauh. -> His / her (honored) office is to the west.
15. Peken nika di kauh. -> That market is to the west.
16. Luhur. -> Up / high (refined).
17. Kedis nika ring luhur. -> That bird is up high.
18. Ida linggih ring luhur. -> He / she (honored) sits up high / in a high place.
19. Genah ipun ring luhur. -> His / her place is up high.
20. Baduwur. -> Up (familiar).
21. Meong nika di baduwur. -> That cat is up high.
22. Cai ngadeg di baduwur? -> Are you standing up there?
23. Buku icange di baduwur. -> My book is up there.
24. Baduwur. -> Up (familiar variant).
25. Kedis ento di baduwur. -> That bird is up high.
26. Nyai ngadeg di baduwur? -> Are you standing up there?
27. Baju nika di baduwur. -> That shirt is up there.
28. Beten. -> Down / under (familiar).
29. Bikul ada di beten. -> There is a mouse under (it).
30. Buku icange di beten. -> My book is down there / under (it).
31. Cai negak di beten? -> Do you sit down there?
32. Ring sor. -> Down / under (refined phrase).
33. Jero ketut wénten ring sor. -> The mouse is under (it).
34. Buku druwen tiang ring sor. -> My book is below.
35. Ipun linggih ring sor. -> He / she sits in the lower place.
36. Ajeng. -> Front / before (refined).
37. Sanggah wénten ring ajeng jero. -> The family shrine is in front of the house.
38. Cicing ida ring ajeng. -> His / her (honored) dog is in front.
39. Tiang negak ring ajeng ida. -> I sit in front of him / her (honored).
40. Harep. -> Front (familiar).
41. Ada cicing di harep umah. -> There is a dog in front of the house.
42. Montor icang di harep. -> My motorbike is in front.
43. Cai ngadeg di harep icang. -> You stand in front of me.
44. Pungkur. -> Back / behind (refined).
45. Merajan wénten ring pungkur. -> The family temple is at the back.
46. Sampi ida ring pungkur jero. -> His / her (honored) cow is behind the house.
47. Tiang negak ring pungkur. -> I sit at the back.
48. Duri. -> Back / behind (familiar).
49. Celeng nika di duri umah. -> That pig is behind the house.
50. Cai negak di duri icang. -> You sit behind me.
51. Ada bikul di duri. -> There is a mouse behind (it).
52. Tengen. -> Right (neutral).
53. Toko nika di tengen. -> That store is on the right.
54. Peken nika ring tengen. -> That market is on the right.
55. Warung nika di tengen. -> That stall is on the right.
56. Kiwa. -> Left (neutral).
57. Sekolah nika di kiwa. -> That school is on the left.
58. Kantor nika ring kiwa. -> That office is on the left.
59. Kiwa, nenten tengen. -> Left, not right.
60. Nampek. -> Near (refined).
61. Jero ida nampek ring pasar. -> His / her (honored) home is near the market.
62. Sekolah ipun nampek. -> His / her school is near.
63. Genah tiang nampek ring jero ida. -> My place is near his / her (honored) home.
64. Paek. -> Near (familiar).
65. Umah icang paek di peken. -> My house is near the market.
66. Cai nongos paek? -> Do you live near here?
67. Sekolah icang paek di jumah. -> My school is near home.
68. Adoh. -> Far (refined).
69. Pasar nika adoh. -> That market is far.
70. Jero ida adoh ring driki. -> His / her (honored) home is far from here.
71. Sekolah ida adoh. -> His / her (honored) school is far.
72. Joh. -> Far (familiar).
73. Peken nika joh. -> That market is far.
74. Carik icang joh. -> My rice field is far.
75. Joh, sing paek. -> Far, not near.
76. Padoh. -> Far (refined variant).
77. Kota nika padoh. -> That city is far.
78. Desa ida padoh ring driki. -> His / her (honored) village is far from here.
79. Segara nika padoh? -> Is that sea far?
80. Tanggu. -> End / edge / side (familiar).
81. Umah icang di tanggu jalan. -> My house is at the end of the road.
82. Warung nika di tanggu rurung. -> That stall is at the end of the alley.
83. Tanggu kangin, ada pura. -> On the east side there is a temple.
84. Tengah. -> Middle / inside (neutral).
85. Ada tukad di tengah alas. -> There is a river in the middle of the forest.
86. Icang negak di tengah. -> I sit in the middle.
87. Kedis ento di tengah carik. -> That bird is in the middle of the rice field.
88. Sisi. -> Outside / side (neutral).
89. Cicing nika di sisi. -> That dog is outside.
90. Icang negak di sisi jero. -> I sit outside the house.
91. Ada warung di sisi jalan. -> There is a stall by the side of the road.
92. Jaba. -> Outside (refined).
93. Asu nika ring jaba. -> That dog is outside.
94. Tiang negak ring jaba jero. -> I sit outside the house.
95. Wénten tamiu ring jaba. -> There is a guest outside.
96. Jalan lurus. -> Go straight (phrase).
97. Jalan lurus, warung nika di tengen. -> Go straight — the stall is on the right.
98. Jalan lurus ka peken. -> Go straight to the market.
99. Jalan lurus, pasar nika ring ajeng. -> Go straight; the market is ahead.
100. Simpangan. -> Intersection.
101. Ada simpangan dini. -> There is an intersection here.
102. Simpangan nika dija? -> Where is that intersection?
103. Toko nika paek di simpangan. -> That store is near the intersection.
104. Dini. -> Here (familiar). [recycled — fresh sentences]
105. Mai, dini ada warung. -> Come — there's a stall here.
106. Icang negak dini. -> I sit here.
107. Dini paek di simpangan. -> Here is near the intersection.
108. Driki. -> Here (refined). [recycled]
109. Mriki, wénten genah becik driki. -> Come here — there is a good place here.
110. Tiang negak driki. -> I sit here.
111. Driki nampek ring pura. -> Here is near the temple.
112. Ditu. -> There (familiar). [recycled]
113. Kema, ditu ada peken. -> Go on — there's a market there.

### Lesson 26: Prepositions

## Section 2 Completion Summary

After Lesson 30, the learner can build **full, natural sentences** combining everything from Sections 1–2:

- **Describe routines:** `Dibi icang bangun semeng, manjus, raris majalan ka peken.` (with Lesson 14 time words and Lesson 30 connectors).
- **Navigate:** ask and give directions with kaja/kelod/kangin/kauh, dija/ring dija, jalan lurus, paek/joh, and all prepositions (di/ring, ka/ke, uling/saking, duwur/beten, ajeng/pungkur, samping, antara).
- **Describe the world:** size, age, beauty, colors, weather, nature — `Gunung nika tegeh gati, lan segara nika pelung.`
- **Express inner life:** feelings, wants, and certainty (`Tiang liang krana tepuk timpal.`).
- **Eat, shop, and pay:** market foods, bargaining (`Dadi kuang?`), prices in thousands (`seket tali`), paying and change.
- **Talk about body and health:** `Sirah icang sakit`, `Ida sungkan weteng`.
- **Dress appropriately:** garments, wearing verbs, clean/dirty.
- **Move around:** vehicles, riding, driving, traffic, tickets.
- **Discuss home and family:** the house compound, rooms, furniture, chores, and the full extended family plus community roles (kelian, pemangku, krama).
- **Connect ideas:** and/but/or, because/therefore, although, until, since, before/after, if, so that — in both registers.

**Ready for Section 3 (A2),** where past (`sampun/suba … taen`), future (`lakar/jagi`), occupations, opinions, ceremonies, comparisons, and storytelling complete the journey to independent conversation.
